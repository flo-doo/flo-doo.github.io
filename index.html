About

Florence X. Doo, MD, MA, is an abdominal radiologist, clinical informaticist, and physician innovator whose work focuses on trustworthy human–AI systems, frontier clinical intelligence, and sustainable AI and medical imaging.

She is an Assistant Professor at the University of Maryland School of Medicine and works across clinical radiology, translational AI, and digital health innovation.
