About

Clinical practice grounds the research.

Florence X. Doo, MD, MA, is an abdominal radiologist, clinical informaticist, clinical AI researcher, and physician innovator. Her work focuses on translating emerging computational technologies into clinical care, with particular attention to trustworthy human–AI systems, frontier clinical intelligence, and sustainability.
